After Dark Studio
=================

This software contains no Berkeley Systems code, artwork, or sound.

After Dark Studio is an independent front end. It reads an After Dark
installation that you already own, on your own machine, at runtime:

  * the engine (ADXPL510.DLL) is loaded from your installation
  * modules (.AD files) are read and run from your installation
  * artwork, palettes and sounds stay on your disk and are never copied

Nothing from After Dark is redistributed with this installer.

You need a licensed copy of After Dark 4 for this software to do anything
useful. If you do not have one, After Dark Studio will start, find no
installation, and offer to let you point it at one.
